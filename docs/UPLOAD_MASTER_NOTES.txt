**Correct upload order for beginner**


Do the upload in this order.


**Upload 1 — Master Data**

Go to:

http://localhost:8000/masters/upload


**Select:**

All Master Data Workbook

**Upload:**

Master Data.xlsx



**Upload 2 — Inventory**

**Select:**

Inventory Master

**Upload:**

Inventory.xlsx



**Upload 3 — Recipe master**

**Select:**

Recipes Data Upload / Recipes \& Costing

Upload:

Master recipes.xlsx



**Upload 4 — Recipe ingredients**

**Select:**

Recipes Data Upload / Recipes \& Costing

**Upload:**

Recipes Ingredients.xlsx
